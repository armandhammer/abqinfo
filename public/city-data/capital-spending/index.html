      Capital improvement spending and projects.

      Current Capital Program#

      • 2025 General Obligation Bond Approved Program Book

        Albuquerque’s approved 2025 General Obligation Bond program describes proposed capital investments across the 2025-2034 decade plan, including project scopes, funding categories, public facilities, infrastructure, and implementation priorities.

      • 2025–2034 Approved Decade Plan Summary (archived PDF)

        Summarizes approved capital allocations across the 2025–2034 decade plan by department and biennial bond cycle, showing long-range funding for streets, drainage, parks, transit, housing, facilities, and public safety.

        Download archived allocation workbook · Official City PDF · Official City workbook

      2027–2036 Planning Cycle#

      • 2027–2036 Decade Plan Funding Allocation Chart (archived PDF)

        Shows preliminary department allocations and plan submissions for the 2027–2036 decade plan across four bond cycles, including streets, drainage, parks, transit, housing, public safety, community facilities, and mandated set-asides.

        The City URL is named as an Excel workbook but currently serves this one-page PDF. Open the official City file.

      Historical Capital Programs#

      • 2013–2022 Decade Plan and 2013 General Obligation Bond Program (archived PDF)

        Presents the Environmental Planning Commission version of the 2013–2022 Albuquerque capital-improvement decade plan and 2013 bond program, with department projects, funding schedules, maps, justifications, and program criteria.

        Official City PDF

      • 2019 General Obligation Bond Program Book (archived PDF)

        Documents the 2019 Albuquerque capital program by bond purpose and department, preserving proposed project scopes, costs, locations, schedules, funding sources, and the broader decade-plan context.

        Official City PDF

      • 2021 General Obligation Bond Approved Program (archived PDF)

        Preserves the approved 2021 Albuquerque bond program, including department allocations, detailed capital-project requests, project purposes, locations, schedules, costs, funding sources, and implementation information.

        Official City PDF

      • 2023 General Obligation Bond Program Book (archived PDF)

        Compiles the 2023 Albuquerque General Obligation Bond program by purpose and department, with project descriptions, locations, justifications, requested funding, schedules, and capital-planning context.

        Official City PDF
