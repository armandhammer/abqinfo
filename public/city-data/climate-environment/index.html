      Climate & Environment

      • Climate Action Plans
      • Greenhouse-Gas Inventory

      City and regional climate plans, greenhouse-gas inventories, and durable performance records relevant to Albuquerque.

      • City Climate Action Plan and Implementation Hub

        Maintains Albuquerque’s current climate-plan overview, implementation reports, inventories, updates, and related Sustainability Office work. Archived records below preserve the planning lineage and measured baseline.

      Climate Action Plans#

      • City of Albuquerque Climate Action Plan (2009)

        Preserves Albuquerque’s first Climate Action Task Force recommendations, covering greenhouse-gas accounting, buildings, renewable energy, neighborhoods, food, waste, behavior change, and transportation before the City’s later equity-centered plan.

        Official City climate-plan hub · Official City PDF

      • Albuquerque Climate Action Plan (2021)

        Sets Albuquerque’s community-scale climate strategy with equity-centered actions for governance, energy, buildings, transportation, waste, economic development, adaptation, health, and accountability, informed by frontline-community engagement and implementation priorities.

        Official City climate-plan hub · Official City PDF

      Greenhouse-Gas Inventory#

      • Central New Mexico Community-Scale Greenhouse Gas Inventory (2023)

        Quantifies 2023 community greenhouse-gas emissions across Central New Mexico, documenting methods, energy, transportation, waste, industrial sources, agriculture, land use, and the baseline used for regional climate planning and progress measurement.

        Official City climate-plan hub · Official City PDF
