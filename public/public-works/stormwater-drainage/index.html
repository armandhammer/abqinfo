      Stormwater & Drainage

      • Hydrology and Development Review
      • Municipal Stormwater Research
      • Historical Rainfall, Runoff, and Water-Quality Data

      Technical standards, studies, models, and public references for Albuquerque’s drainage, stormwater, erosion-control, and municipal storm-sewer systems.

      Hydrology and Development Review#

      • AHYMO-S4-R2 Hydrologic Model User’s Manual (2018)

        Documents Albuquerque’s official arid-lands hydrologic modeling software, including watershed inputs, rainfall, infiltration, routing, detention facilities, sediment, output interpretation, examples, and procedures used in local drainage analysis and development review.

        Official City Hydrology Section · Official City PDF

      • City of Albuquerque Construction Site Manual (2018)

        Sets illustrated inspection standards for construction-site sediment and erosion controls, vehicle tracking, inlet protection, channels, housekeeping, washout, debris, street cuts, curb work, stabilization, and maintenance of required best practices.

        Official City development-review document library · Official City PDF

      Municipal Stormwater Research#

      • AMAFCA/Albuquerque MS4 Floatable and Gross Pollutant Study (2005)

        Analyzes litter and gross pollutants entering Albuquerque’s municipal stormwater system, identifies sources and drainage conditions, evaluates reduction strategies and structural controls, and establishes a coordinated AMAFCA-City program framework.

        Official City Street and Storm Maintenance page · Official City PDF

      Historical Rainfall, Runoff, and Water-Quality Data#

      • Rainfall, Runoff, and Water-Quality Data for the Albuquerque Metropolitan Area, Water Year 2003 (2005 archived PDF)

        Preserves USGS and City monitoring data for rainfall, runoff, channel flow, and stormwater quality across the Albuquerque metropolitan area, including methods, station records, hydrographs, and water-year 2003 results.

        Official City Street and Storm Maintenance page · Official City PDF

      • Rainfall, Runoff, and Water-Quality Data for the Albuquerque Metropolitan Area, Water Year 2004 (2006 archived PDF)

        Continues the metropolitan rainfall, runoff, channel-flow, and stormwater-quality record with monitoring methods, station data, hydrographs, precipitation, discharge, and water-quality results for water year 2004.

        Official City Street and Storm Maintenance page · Official City PDF
