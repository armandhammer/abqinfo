      Rider Information#

      • ABQ RIDE: Routes and Schedules

        The City’s current route, schedule, detour, and trip-planning information. Consult the official service page before a trip because operational details can change.

      Uptown Connect#

      • Uptown Connect Project Page

        Tracks the City’s transit-centered redevelopment of the Uptown Transit Center, including design and construction progress, the transit plaza, affordable and market-rate housing, commercial space, sustainability features, and the anticipated 2026–2028 delivery schedule.

      ABQ RIDE Forward Network Plan#

      • Official ABQ RIDE Forward Project Page

        Tracks the finalized bus-network plan, phased implementation, current project status, official reports, route concepts, and project contact information maintained by the City.

      • Final Recovery Network Plan (June 2025)

        Defines the finalized route network, frequencies, service spans, access changes, public-input response, and phased next steps for rebuilding and modernizing Albuquerque bus service. Official City PDF

      • Final Recovery Network Map (2025)

        Maps the finalized ABQ RIDE Forward network by route and frequency, providing a printable overview of planned fixed-route service and demand-response areas. Official City image

      Planning History#

      • Phase III Recovery Network Report (May 2024)

        Presents the draft recovery network, proposed route and frequency changes, service assumptions, access analysis, demographic outcomes, and recommendations later refined into the final plan. Official City PDF

      • Phase III Engagement Summary (November 2024)

        Documents community and stakeholder feedback on the draft recovery network, including outreach, survey results, route-specific comments, respondent characteristics, and requested changes. Official City PDF

      • Phase II Concepts Report (March 2023)

        Compares contrasting high-ridership and high-coverage bus-network concepts, explaining route structures, service tradeoffs, access outcomes, transit markets, and choices considered during redesign. Official City PDF

      • Phase II Engagement Summary (August 2023)

        Summarizes public preferences between high-ridership and high-coverage concepts, including surveys, focus groups, stakeholder input, demographic analysis, and priorities for evening and weekend service. Official City PDF

      • Phase I Existing Conditions Report (August 2022)

        Analyzes Albuquerque transit markets, network and route performance, ridership, service frequency, access, equity, zero fares, worker shortages, and fundamental network-design choices. Official City PDF

      • Phase I Engagement Summary (February 2023)

        Records early community priorities and transit tradeoffs gathered through surveys, focus groups, pop-up events, workshops, meetings, presentations, and outreach to riders and underserved populations. Official City PDF

      • 2022 ABQ RIDE On-Board Rider Survey

        Profiles weekday and weekend riders, travel patterns, transfers, trip purposes, service use, demographics, automobile access, and priorities for improving Albuquerque bus service, providing the rider baseline used during ABQ RIDE Forward and later zero-fare policy analysis. City resolution citing the original survey

      Project contact: Andrew de Garmo, Principal Planner, ABQ RIDE — adegarmo@cabq.gov — 505-724-3109.

      Montaño Intermodal Center Planning History#

      • Montaño Intermodal Center Project Application (2010 archived PDF)

        Documents the City’s federal funding application for a Montaño intermodal center, connecting Rail Runner, local bus service, walking, bicycling, housing, employment, neighborhood access, and coordinated transportation investment.

        Official City PDF · Official City transit document collection

      Earlier Transit Investment Studies#

      • Albuquerque Streetcar Evaluation Summary Report (2008 archived PDF)

        Evaluates a proposed modern streetcar through Albuquerque’s historic and cultural core, examining ridership, operations, costs, land-use and economic-development benefits, funding, implementation, and comparisons with peer systems.

        Official City PDF

      • Albuquerque Streetcar Evaluation Appendices (2008 archived PDF)

        Preserves the streetcar evaluation’s supporting technical record, including peer systems, operating costs, ridership, preliminary cost analysis, land-use benefits, funding analysis, earlier HDR work, and the February 2008 introductory presentation.

        Official City PDF

      • Albuquerque Streetcar Cost and Ridership Analysis (2008 archived PDF)

        Reviews the proposed Albuquerque modern streetcar’s capital and operating costs, ridership assumptions, peer systems, service design, land-use forecasts, and potential development impacts as the baseline for the broader 2008 rail-investment evaluation.

        Official City PDF · Official City project archive

      Open Data#

      • ABQ RIDE Open Data

        The City publishes General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) data for applications and analysis. The live feed is preferable to an archived copy for current service information.
