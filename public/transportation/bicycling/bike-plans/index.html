    City bicycle planning documents and master plans, including current and previous network plans and supporting materials.

    Current Bike Plan#

    • 2024 Bikeway and Trail Facilities Plan
      • Establishes Albuquerque’s current bicycle and trail network vision, policies, facility recommendations, project priorities, and implementation framework for safer, better-connected active transportation.
      • Bicycle and Trail Crossings Guide
        • Provides design guidance for bicycle and trail crossings, helping practitioners select treatments that improve visibility, clarify priority, and reduce conflicts at roadway intersections.
      • Appendix A – Planning and Policy Framework
        • Summarizes the adopted plans, policies, and regulatory context that shaped the 2024 plan’s goals, network recommendations, priorities, and implementation approach.
      • Appendix B – Community Survey Summary (Spring 2023)
        • Reports responses from the spring 2023 community survey, documenting residents’ bicycling and trail experiences, concerns, priorities, and desired improvements across Albuquerque.
      • Appendix C – Project Priorities Survey Map Results (Fall 2023)
        • Compiles mapped results from the fall 2023 priorities survey, showing where participants identified desired bikeway, crossing, connection, and trail improvements.
      • Appendix D – Bikeway Evaluation Process
        • Explains the plan’s repeatable method for evaluating proposed bikeways, comparing corridor conditions, network needs, feasibility, and project priorities across Albuquerque.
      • Appendix E – Bike Boulevard Toolkit
        • Describes planning and design tools for low-stress bicycle boulevards, including traffic calming, crossing improvements, wayfinding, and strategies for managing motor-vehicle volumes.
      • Appendix F – Priority Projects Tables (Jan 2025)
        • Lists the plan’s priority bicycle and trail projects as of January 2025, organizing recommended improvements for implementation and future programming.
      • Appendix G – Project Profiles
        • Presents detailed profiles for selected priority projects, describing corridor needs, recommended facilities, implementation considerations, and each project’s role in the network.
      • Appendix H – Multiuse Trail Considerations (2015 Plan)
        • Preserves relevant multiuse-trail planning and design considerations from the 2015 plan for continued technical reference alongside the updated 2024 recommendations.
      • Appendix I – Bollard Assessment (2013)
        • Documents the City’s 2013 assessment of multiuse-trail bollards, including operational and safety considerations that inform decisions about access-control treatments on trails.

    Previous Bike Plans#

    • 2015 Bikeways and Trails Facilities Plan
      • The previous adopted facilities plan established the bicycle and trail network, policies, design guidance, and project priorities that preceded the current 2024 plan.
    • 2011 Albuquerque Bikeways and Trails Master Plan Update
      • Updated Albuquerque’s earlier bicycle and trail planning framework, documenting existing conditions, proposed networks, facility guidance, and implementation priorities at that time.
    • 2000 Albuquerque Comprehensive On-Street Bicycle Plan
      • Established a citywide framework for Albuquerque’s on-street bicycle facilities, identifying network needs, recommended routes, design approaches, policies, and implementation priorities.
    • 1993 Trails and Bikeways Facility Plan
      • Documents Albuquerque’s early citywide approach to developing connected trail and bikeway facilities, preserving the historical basis for subsequent network planning.

    Related Bicycle Policy#

    • City Council Resolution R-07-268: Bike Boulevards (2007)
      • Designates Mountain Road, Silver Avenue, and 14th Street bike-boulevard routes; appropriates up to $400,000 for design, engineering, and initial implementation; and directs amendments to the Comprehensive Bikeway Facilities Plan.
