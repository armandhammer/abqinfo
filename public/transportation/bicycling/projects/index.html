      Bicycle infrastructure projects.

      Projects dated from 2021 through 2026 are grouped as current. Projects from 2020 or earlier are grouped as past, avoiding uncertain judgments about whether construction or closeout is still active.

      Current Projects (2021–2026)#

      Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Avenue Separated Bicycle Lane Pilot#

      • City Project Page

        Tracks the separated-lane pilot on Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Avenue, including project limits, safety purpose, installation approach, evaluation period, frequently asked questions, and relationship to the adopted Bikeway and Trail Facilities Plan.

      Questions or comments about this pilot: Valerie Hermanson at DMD, vhermanson@cabq.gov

      Buena Vista Bike Boulevard#

      Location: Buena Vista Dr SE, from Central Ave SE to Gibson Blvd SE

      • Buena Vista Bike Boulevard 60% Plans

        Shows the 60-percent design for bicycle-boulevard improvements along Buena Vista Drive, including traffic-calming, crossing, signing, pavement-marking, and corridor layout details.

      Questions or comments email Paul Olson at DMD polson@cabq.gov

      Moon Street Cycletrack / Bike Boulevard#

      Location: Moon Street NE, between Academy Rd NE and Spain Rd NE

      • Moon Street NE Cycletrack Plans

        Documents the planned Moon Street restriping and cycletrack layout between Academy and Spain, including bicycle facilities, lane configuration, markings, and intersection treatments.

      Questions or comments email Melissa Roseman at DMD melissaroseman@cabq.gov

      Past Projects (2020 and Earlier)#

      Uptown Pedestrian, Bicycle, and Wayfinding Program#

      • Uptown Pedestrian and Bicycle Plan and Wayfinding Program (2008 archived PDF)

        Develops an Uptown walking, bicycling, shuttle, parking, circulation, public-space, branding, and wayfinding framework intended to help the district evolve into a compact, mixed-use, pedestrian-oriented activity center.

        Official City PDF · Official City transit document collection

      San Pedro Drive and Mile-Hi#

      • San Pedro Drive Bike Facility Assessment (2014 archived PDF)

        Evaluates a San Pedro Drive road diet and bicycle facilities through traffic volumes, intersection operations, future growth, crash history, access, parking, transit, design alternatives, and public feedback.

        Official City PDF · Official City project page
