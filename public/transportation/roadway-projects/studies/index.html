    State highway studies affecting Albuquerque#

    • I-40 West Corridor Study Executive Summary (2024)

      This NMDOT executive summary covers the I-40 Phase I-A/B corridor study from Arizona to Albuquerque, mileposts 0 through 150. The NMDOT project page remains the source for project updates and supporting materials.

    • I-40 Phase I-A/B Corridor Study (2024)

      Presents NMDOT’s signed Phase I-A/B analysis of I-40 from Arizona to Albuquerque, documenting public involvement, existing conditions, traffic and safety, alternatives screening, preferred improvements, costs, operational enhancements, and implementation.

    • I-40 Highway Operations Improvement Plan (2024)

      Provides NMDOT’s 25-year implementation roadmap for I-40 from Arizona to Albuquerque’s Atrisco Vista interchange, covering preferred improvements, design guidance, maintenance, incident management, priority projects, and phasing.

    • I-40 Conceptual Alternatives Plan Sheets (2024)

      Contains 139 preliminary plan sheets mapping conceptual I-40 improvements from Arizona to Albuquerque, including reconstruction limits, typical sections, crossovers, bridge constraints, curves, ramps, and pavement conditions.

    • Big I Operations Study

      NMDOT’s Big I study page describes work at the I-25/I-40 interchange and links to the project’s interactive material. It belongs under studies until a defined implementation project and status are established.

    • I-25 S-Curve Area Study

      Documents NMDOT’s evaluation of safety and mobility improvements between Avenida Cesar Chavez and Lomas Boulevard, including alignment alternatives, interchange concepts, screening results, public meetings, and draft recommendations.

    • I-25 Gibson Interchange Reconstruction Project

      Tracks design development for replacing the Gibson interchange and improving I-25 between Sunport and Avenida Cesar Chavez, including braided ramps, frontage roads, multimodal connections, environmental review, and public materials.

    • I-25 Bobby Foster and Mesa del Sol Interchange Study

      Covers NMDOT’s Phase I-A/B study of I-25 from the Rio Grande bridges to Sunport Boulevard, evaluating future interchange, corridor, access, safety, and capacity improvements serving Mesa del Sol.

    • NM 556/Tramway Boulevard Public Engagement Summary (November 2025)

      Summarizes NMDOT’s 2025 public engagement for NM 556/Tramway Boulevard, including meeting outreach, roughly 180 comments, recurring safety and operations concerns, agency responses, and next steps.

    • NMDOT NM 556/Tramway Boulevard Corridor Study

      Tracks the active Albuquerque corridor study from Central Avenue to Live Oak Road, including current findings, April 2026 meeting resources, safety priorities, recommended strategies, and the study timeline.
