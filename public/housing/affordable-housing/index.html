    Affordable Housing

    • Plans and Data

    Affordable housing programs and data.

    Plans and Data#

    • ABC Comprehensive Plan, Chapter 9: Housing (2025 Update)

      The housing element of the adopted Albuquerque/Bernalillo County Comprehensive Plan sets policies for housing choice, affordability, neighborhood stability, fair housing, infrastructure, and coordinated implementation across the city and unincorporated county.

    • Albuquerque Region Housing Needs Assessment (2024)

      Prepared for the City of Albuquerque and Mid-Region Council of Governments, this regional assessment documents current housing needs, production needs, vacant-land capacity, growth, fair-share analysis, demographic trends, and conditions affecting housing affordability across the Albuquerque region.

    • City of Albuquerque 2023-2027 Consolidated Plan and 2023 Action Plan

      The City’s HUD Consolidated Plan identifies housing, homelessness, community-development, and fair-housing needs for 2023-2027. Its accompanying 2023 Action Plan describes the first year’s planned use of federal housing and community-development resources.
