    Current ordinance and zoning tools#

    • Integrated Development Ordinance — effective May 6, 2026

      The City’s current Integrated Development Ordinance establishes zoning, development standards, and review procedures for Albuquerque. This preserved original is the effective May 2026 release; the City IDO page retains archived updates and legislative history.

    • Interactive IDO zoning map

      Search an address or parcel and inspect the mapped zoning layer. Map results should be checked against the effective IDO and City review staff for a site-specific determination.

    • City zoning and land-use maps

      The City’s public map library includes the Zone Atlas, address atlas, council districts, land use, neighborhood associations, and printable reference maps.
