      Area & Sector Plans

      • Southwest Mesa and Volcano Area
      • Fourth Street and Montaño

      Historical area, sector, and community plans that explain how Albuquerque neighborhoods, infrastructure, land use, and transportation developed. Many no longer control zoning, but remain important planning-history records.

      • City Area & Sector Development Plan Library

        Maintains the City’s current reference index for adopted historical plans. ABQInfo preserves selected complete plans and adoption records whose context remains independently useful.

      Southwest Mesa and Volcano Area#

      • Southwest Area Plan (Bernalillo County 2001; Albuquerque 2002)

        Preserves the jointly adopted Southwest Area Plan covering growth, land use, transportation, infrastructure, community form, environmental resources, public facilities, economic development, implementation, and City and County responsibilities.

        Official City plan library · Official City PDF

      • Volcano Trails Sector Development Plan (2011)

        Preserves the adopted Volcano Trails framework for village-center and residential development, open space, transportation, trails, infrastructure, urban design, zoning, implementation, and protection of the volcanic landscape.

        Official City plan library · Official City PDF

      • Volcano Trails Sector Development Plan Adoption Resolution R-11-211 (2011)

        Records City Council’s adoption and zoning actions for the 446-acre Volcano Trails plan area, including village center, residential districts, open space, boundaries, findings, and implementation conditions.

        Official City project archive · Official City PDF

      Fourth Street and Montaño#

      • Fourth Street and Montaño Community Visioning Report (2004)

        Documents community priorities for the Fourth Street and Montaño area, including traffic, pedestrian safety, drainage, neighborhood character, commercial development, public space, land use, and coordinated improvement strategies.

        Official City project archive · Official City PDF
