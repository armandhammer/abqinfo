      Metropolitan Redevelopment Plans

      • Current Downtown Plan
      • Historical and Retained Area Plans

      Plans and historical records for designated Metropolitan Redevelopment Areas, where the City uses public investment and redevelopment tools to support housing, infrastructure, business activity, and neighborhood revitalization.

      • City Metropolitan Redevelopment Areas

        Maintains the City’s current index of designated areas, maps, plans, goals, and redevelopment information. The archived plans below preserve records that may be amended, renamed, consolidated, or removed from the live collection.

      Current Downtown Plan#

      • Downtown 2050 Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Plan (2025 archived PDF)

        Sets the current 25-year framework for Downtown housing, transportation, economic development, public spaces, cultural identity, land use, public investment, redevelopment incentives, implementation, and the expanded metropolitan redevelopment area.

        Official City redevelopment-area page · Official City PDF

      Historical and Retained Area Plans#

      • Downtown 2025 Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (2004; retained 2017; superseded 2025 archived PDF)

        Preserves the predecessor to Downtown 2050, documenting two decades of downtown land-use, transportation, urban-design, housing, economic-development, public-space, implementation, boundary, and adoption policy for historical comparison.

        Current official Downtown redevelopment-area page · Historical official City PDF

      • East Gateway Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Plan (2014 archived PDF)

        Guides revitalization of Albuquerque’s eastern Central Avenue gateway through public improvements, housing, business support, streetscape, transportation, safety, community identity, redevelopment tools, funding criteria, and implementation priorities.

        Official City redevelopment-area page · Official City PDF

      • Old Albuquerque High School Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (1984)

        Documents the redevelopment framework for the former Albuquerque High School property, coordinating historic preservation, renovation, public improvements, private investment, and reuse of a prominent Huning Highland landmark.

        Official City redevelopment-area index · Official City PDF

      • Central/Highland/Upper Nob Hill Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (2005)

        Guides reinvestment along Central Avenue through business revitalization, housing, higher-density mixed use, pedestrian improvements, neighborhood stabilization, and coordinated public and private development in the Near Heights.

        Official City redevelopment-area page · Official City PDF

      • Clayton Heights Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Plan (2010)

        Establishes a redevelopment strategy for the Clayton Heights gateway neighborhood, addressing land use, housing, infrastructure, streets, economic conditions, opportunity sites, community priorities, and implementation tools.

        Official City redevelopment-area index · Official City PDF

      • Coronado Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Plan (2016)

        Plans environmental remediation, public-right-of-way improvements, and mixed-use redevelopment of the former industrial property, supporting new housing, jobs, retail, entertainment, connectivity, safety, drainage, and a northern gateway identity.

        Official City redevelopment-area page · Official City PDF

      • Del Rey Metropolitan Redevelopment Project Plan (2006)

        Records the negotiated redevelopment plan for the Del Rey area, including project boundaries, housing and land-development conditions, resident coordination, public actions, statutory findings, and implementation responsibilities.

        Official City redevelopment-area index · Official City PDF

      • McClellan Park Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (1984; amended through 2002; retained 2017 archived PDF)

        Preserves the former McClellan Park redevelopment framework, its downtown employment and residential-transition goals, boundary and project amendments, adoption record, and later consolidation into the Downtown redevelopment area.

        Current official Downtown redevelopment-area page · Historical official City PDF

      • Near Heights Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Plan (2000 archived PDF)

        Preserves the original Near Heights redevelopment strategy for commercial corridors, small businesses, jobs, neighborhood stabilization, affordable housing, public improvements, community identity, and investment around Central and nearby districts.

        Official City redevelopment-area page · Official City PDF

      • Near Heights Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Expansion Plan (2010 archived PDF)

        Extends the Near Heights redevelopment strategy into the Eastern-Alvarado area, addressing commercial revitalization, neighborhood stability, housing, jobs, multicultural identity, public improvements, tax-increment financing, and implementation tools.

        Official City redevelopment-area page · Official City PDF

      • North Fourth Street Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (2010; retained 2017 archived PDF)

        Preserves the retained redevelopment framework for North Fourth Street, covering corridor identity, land use, transportation, business development, neighborhoods, public investment, implementation, and the plan’s transition from sector-plan regulations.

        Official City redevelopment-area index · Official City PDF

      • Sawmill/Wells Park Community Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Plan (2005 archived PDF)

        Sets community-led redevelopment priorities for mixed-income housing, walkability, cultural diversity, education, local business, wealth building, transportation, land use, public investment, and implementation in Sawmill and Wells Park.

        Official City redevelopment-area page · Official City PDF

      • South Broadway Neighborhoods Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (1986; Updated Through 2018)

        Preserves the long-running South Broadway redevelopment framework, including neighborhood conditions, housing, commercial corridors, industry, community facilities, transportation, historic character, public improvements, adopted amendments, and implementation history.

        Official City redevelopment-area page · Official City PDF

      • South Barelas Industrial Park Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (1992)

        Promotes environmentally compatible industrial growth beside Barelas, covering business expansion, vacant-land development, City property disposition, industrial-park image, local hiring, code enforcement, transportation access, and infrastructure improvements.

        Official City redevelopment-area page · Official City PDF

      • St. Joseph Hospital Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (1979; Updated Through 2018)

        Preserves the redevelopment record for the former St. Joseph Hospital and Civic Auditorium area, including renovation projects, later development areas, amendments, and the 2017 transition from sector plan to retained MRA plan.

        Official City redevelopment-area index · Official City PDF

      • Tingley Beach Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Project I Plan (1983)

        Preserves the 1983 Project I redevelopment plan for the beach property at West Central Avenue and the Rio Grande, documenting the statutory framework, site conditions, public actions, and proposed reuse.

        Official City redevelopment-area index · Official City PDF

      • West Central Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Plan (2004)

        Guides revitalization along West Central through economic development, streetscape and appearance improvements, opportunity-site design, industrial employment, neighborhood retail, mixed-use development, housing, and connections to the Rio Grande Bosque.

        Official City redevelopment-area page · Official City PDF
