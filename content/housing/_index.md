Housing development, zoning, and land use data.

This section combines adopted housing policy, current housing-need evidence, the legal development framework, and live activity data. A plan may describe policy and need, while a permit or project record describes a different stage of development; the pages keep those roles separate.

For the core policy, see [Housing Pipeline]({{< relref "housing-pipeline.md" >}}). For regulations and site research, start with [Zoning & IDO]({{< relref "zoning-ido.md" >}}) and [Development Process]({{< relref "development-process.md" >}}).
