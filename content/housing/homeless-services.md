Homeless services and shelter data.

## Current resources and transparency

- [Emergency shelters, day shelters, and street outreach](https://www.cabq.gov/health-housing-homelessness/homelessness/homeless-shelters-outreach)

  The City maintains this service directory for emergency overnight shelter, day shelter, and street-outreach options. Availability and intake requirements can change; the page directs people needing help to call 311 or the relevant provider.

- [Shelter resources and encampment transparency](https://www.cabq.gov/health-housing-homelessness/homelessness/encampment-data)

  This City page links current shelter-resource information, the Shelter Connect dashboard, and publicly reported encampment-response data. Reported locations are not a substitute for on-the-ground verification.
