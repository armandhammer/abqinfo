City bicycle planning documents and master plans, including current and previous network plans and supporting materials.

## Current Bike Plan

- [2024 Bikeway and Trail Facilities Plan](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan.pdf)
  - Establishes Albuquerque's current bicycle and trail network vision, policies, facility recommendations, project priorities, and implementation framework for safer, better-connected active transportation.
  - [Bicycle and Trail Crossings Guide](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan-coa-bicycle-and-trail-crossings-guide.pdf)
    - Provides design guidance for bicycle and trail crossings, helping practitioners select treatments that improve visibility, clarify priority, and reduce conflicts at roadway intersections.
  - [Appendix A – Planning and Policy Framework](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan-appendix-a-planning-and-policy-framework.pdf)
    - Summarizes the adopted plans, policies, and regulatory context that shaped the 2024 plan's goals, network recommendations, priorities, and implementation approach.
  - [Appendix B – Community Survey Summary (Spring 2023)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan-appendix-b-community-survey-summary-spring-2023.pdf)
    - Reports responses from the spring 2023 community survey, documenting residents' bicycling and trail experiences, concerns, priorities, and desired improvements across Albuquerque.
  - [Appendix C – Project Priorities Survey Map Results (Fall 2023)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan-appendix-c-project-priorities-survey-map-results-fall-2023.pdf)
    - Compiles mapped results from the fall 2023 priorities survey, showing where participants identified desired bikeway, crossing, connection, and trail improvements.
  - [Appendix D – Bikeway Evaluation Process](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan-appendix-d-bikeway-evaluation-process.pdf)
    - Explains the plan's repeatable method for evaluating proposed bikeways, comparing corridor conditions, network needs, feasibility, and project priorities across Albuquerque.
  - [Appendix E – Bike Boulevard Toolkit](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan-appendix-e-bike-boulevard-toolkit.pdf)
    - Describes planning and design tools for low-stress bicycle boulevards, including traffic calming, crossing improvements, wayfinding, and strategies for managing motor-vehicle volumes.
  - [Appendix F – Priority Projects Tables (Jan 2025)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan-appendix-f-priority-projects-tables-january-2025.pdf)
    - Lists the plan's priority bicycle and trail projects as of January 2025, organizing recommended improvements for implementation and future programming.
  - [Appendix G – Project Profiles](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan-appendix-g-project-profiles.pdf)
    - Presents detailed profiles for selected priority projects, describing corridor needs, recommended facilities, implementation considerations, and each project's role in the network.
  - [Appendix H – Multiuse Trail Considerations (2015 Plan)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan-appendix-h-additional-considerations-for-multiuse-trails-from-2015-plan.pdf)
    - Preserves relevant multiuse-trail planning and design considerations from the 2015 plan for continued technical reference alongside the updated 2024 recommendations.
  - [Appendix I – Bollard Assessment (2013)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2024-albuquerque-bike-plan-appendix-i-multiuse-trail-bollard-assessment-parks-and-recreation-2013.pdf)
    - Documents the City's 2013 assessment of multiuse-trail bollards, including operational and safety considerations that inform decisions about access-control treatments on trails.

### Interactive plan companions

- [Bikeways & Trails 2024 Conditions](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/4cd6fe3cb2d04a5c895b3791bc35ccd3)

  Explains the existing-condition analysis behind Albuquerque's 2024 Bikeway and Trail Facilities Plan, including facility gaps, bicycle traffic stress, comfort, equity, Vision Zero, and underlying interactive data.

- [Existing & Proposed Network](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/cbf571a80aef4738a8dbe89036c57a48)

  Presents the existing and proposed bikeway network supporting Albuquerque's adopted 2024 plan, linking interactive maps with project strategies, prioritization, and the plan's broader recommendations.

## Previous Bike Plans
- [2015 Bikeways and Trails Facilities Plan](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2015-bikeways-and-trails-facilities-plan.pdf)
  - The previous adopted facilities plan established the bicycle and trail network, policies, design guidance, and project priorities that preceded the current 2024 plan.
- [2011 Albuquerque Bikeways and Trails Master Plan Update](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2011-albuquerque-bikeways-and-trails-master-plan-update.pdf)
  - Updated Albuquerque's earlier bicycle and trail planning framework, documenting existing conditions, proposed networks, facility guidance, and implementation priorities at that time.
- [2000 Albuquerque Comprehensive On-Street Bicycle Plan](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/2000-albuquerque-comprehensive-on-street-bicycle-plan.pdf)
  - Established a citywide framework for Albuquerque's on-street bicycle facilities, identifying network needs, recommended routes, design approaches, policies, and implementation priorities.
- [1993 Trails and Bikeways Facility Plan](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/bicycling/bike-plans/1993-trails-and-bikeways-facility-plan.pdf)
  - Documents Albuquerque's early citywide approach to developing connected trail and bikeway facilities, preserving the historical basis for subsequent network planning.

## Related Bicycle Policy

- [City Council Resolution R-07-268: Bike Boulevards (2007)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/transportation-plans/2007-city-council-bike-boulevards-resolution-r-07-268.pdf)
  - Designates Mountain Road, Silver Avenue, and 14th Street bike-boulevard routes; appropriates up to $400,000 for design, engineering, and initial implementation; and directs amendments to the Comprehensive Bikeway Facilities Plan.
