Bicycle infrastructure projects.

Project pages are organized by the strongest available status evidence, not by an assumed construction schedule. Each entry should identify its corridor, lead agency, dated source material, and whether it is active, planned, or complete.

Use [Current Projects]({{< relref "current.md" >}}) for active, funded, programmed, or formally advancing work. Completed-project records will receive a separate page when substantive final documents are available.
