---
title: "Roadway Projects"
---

Street and roadway infrastructure projects.

Project status is organized deliberately: **Current Projects** are active or accompanied by current implementation materials; **Studies** are alternatives analysis or project-development work; **Completed Projects** retain final reference material. State highway projects are included only when they materially affect Albuquerque.

## State highway project gateway

- [NMDOT District 3](https://www.dot.nm.gov/contact-us/districts/district-3/)

  District 3 is NMDOT's Albuquerque-area office and the authoritative starting point for state-highway contacts, construction information, maintenance responsibilities, and district projects affecting the metropolitan area.
