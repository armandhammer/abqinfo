Transportation projects, planning, and data for Albuquerque.

This section separates long-range plans and funding from current projects, while preserving the lead agency and source date for each item. City streets, regional programs, Bernalillo County facilities, and state highways are intentionally kept distinct.

Start with [Transportation Plans]({{< relref "transportation-plans.md" >}}) for the adopted regional framework and current improvement program. Use Roadway Projects, Bicycling, and Transit for mode- or corridor-specific information.

## Roadside participation

- [NMDOT Adopt a Highway](https://www.dot.nm.gov/adopt-a-highway)

  Explains how Albuquerque-area individuals, organizations, and businesses can adopt a state-highway segment, including cleanup frequency, safety coordination, NMDOT-provided equipment and signs, and the District Three contact.
