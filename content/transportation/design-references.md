## Toronto-style "Tall Curbs" for Bike Lane Protection

High-profile concrete curb barriers used for physical separation and protection of bicycle lanes and other multimodal facilities.

* [Toronto-style Tall Curb Engineering Drawings](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/toronto-tall-curb-design-specs.pdf)

### Related Notes

* Intended primarily for quick-build or retrofit protected bicycle lane applications.
* Taller than standard curb elements and designed to discourage vehicle encroachment.
* May also be used for pedestrian refuge protection, intersection hardening, and traffic calming applications.
