## City Public Works Construction Standards

- [Current City Development Review and Construction Standards Directory](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/DevelopmentReviewServices/DRC/)

  Provides the City's maintained source directory for public-works specifications, standard drawings, technical guidance, and related construction requirements. Check this directory for revisions newer than the preserved editions below.

### Standard Specifications

- [Section 000: General Conditions (archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/cabq-standard-specifications-section-000-general-conditions.pdf)

  Establishes the contractual definitions, responsibilities, administration, changes, payments, acceptance, warranties, and other general conditions governing City public-works construction contracts and projects.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/DevelopmentReviewServices/DRC/section%20000%20General%20Conditions%20%20combined.pdf)

- [Section 100: Materials (archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/cabq-standard-specifications-section-100-materials.pdf)

  Defines material requirements and testing standards used across City public-works construction, including aggregate, concrete, asphalt, pipe, metals, signs, pavement markings, and related products.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/DevelopmentReviewServices/DRC/section%20100%20materials%20combined.pdf)

- [Section 200: Earthwork (archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/cabq-standard-specifications-section-200-earthwork.pdf)

  Covers clearing, excavation, embankment, subgrade preparation, compaction, and related earthwork construction requirements within public rights-of-way, easements, and municipal open areas.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/DevelopmentReviewServices/DRC/Section%20200%20Earthwork%20Combined%20Matirals.pdf)

- [Section 300: Streets and Related Work (archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/cabq-standard-specifications-section-300-streets-related-work.pdf)

- [Section 500: Structures (archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/cabq-standard-specifications-section-500-structures.pdf)

## NMDOT Design and Planning Guidance

Reference guidance for quick-build transportation and multimodal safety projects.

- [NMDOT Quick Build Guide](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/nmdot-quick-build-guide.pdf)

  Introduces quick-build project planning, design, materials, public engagement, installation, and evaluation practices for delivering lower-cost multimodal safety improvements on New Mexico streets.

- [NMDOT Complete Streets Strategic Plan (2025 archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/nmdot-complete-streets-strategic-plan-2025.pdf)

  Sets NMDOT's statewide framework for routinely planning, designing, funding, constructing, operating, and maintaining transportation facilities that safely serve people walking, bicycling, using transit, driving, and moving freight.

  [Official NMDOT PDF](https://api.realfile.rtsclients.com/PublicFiles/f260a66b364d453e91ff9b3fedd494dc/00d5917e-f1ce-444c-899a-630cc65fdc15/2025_07_NMDOT%20Complete%20Streets%20Strategic%20Plan%20-%20Final.pdf) · [Official NMDOT planning page](https://www.dot.nm.gov/planning-research-multimodal-and-safety/planning-division/multimodal-planning-and-programs-bureau/)

- [NMDOT Functional Classification System Guide (2021 archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/nmdot-functional-classification-system-guide-2021.pdf)

  Explains New Mexico roadway functional classifications, why classifications matter, how boundaries are determined, agency responsibilities, review cycles, and procedures for requesting classification changes.

  [Official NMDOT PDF](https://api.realfile.rtsclients.com/PublicFiles/f260a66b364d453e91ff9b3fedd494dc/82ca0d17-f2da-4325-8a0c-b18e8611c584/Functional%20Classification%20Guide.pdf) · [Official NMDOT planning page](https://www.dot.nm.gov/planning-research-multimodal-and-safety/planning-division/multimodal-planning-and-programs-bureau/)

## Toronto-Style "Tall Curbs" for Bike Lane Protection

High-profile concrete curb barriers used for physical separation and protection of bicycle lanes and other multimodal facilities.

- [Toronto-Style Tall Curb Engineering Drawings](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/toronto-tall-curb-design-specs.pdf)

  Provides engineering drawings and dimensional details for the high-profile concrete curb units used to create physical separation between bicycle lanes and motor-vehicle traffic.

- [Tall Curb Spec Sheet](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/tall-curb-spec-sheet-10-13-2025.pdf)

  Summarizes the standard tall-curb unit's geometry and construction specifications for designers, contractors, and reviewers planning protected bicycle-lane separation treatments on Albuquerque projects.

- [Tall Curb End Spec Sheet](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/tall-curb-end-spec-sheet-10-13-2025.pdf)

  Documents the geometry and construction requirements for terminal curb units, which provide a defined transition at the beginning or end of a tall-curb installation.

- [Tall Curb Full Installation Procedures](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/tall-curb-full-installation-procedures.pdf)

  Presents the complete field-installation sequence for tall-curb barriers, including layout, pavement preparation, positioning, anchoring, and checks needed for a consistent finished installation.

- [Tall Curb Anchor Pin Installation Guidelines](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/design-references/tall-curb-anchor-pin-installation-guidelines-10-6-25.pdf)

  Gives focused field guidance for installing the anchor pins that secure tall-curb units to pavement, supporting consistent placement and durable protected-lane installations.
