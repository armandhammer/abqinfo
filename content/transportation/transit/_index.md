Public transit services and data.

Use the service pages for current rider information and the Transportation Plans section for regional transit planning. Schedules, fares, alerts, and eligibility rules change frequently, so this site links to the agencies that maintain them rather than duplicating them.

## Statewide planning context

- [Connect New Mexico: Statewide Public Transportation Plan (May 2025)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/transit/nmdot-statewide-public-transportation-plan-2025.pdf)

  NMDOT’s final statewide public transportation plan establishes the broader policy and investment context for transit providers, riders, and connections affecting Albuquerque. It supplements local and regional plans; it does not replace ABQ RIDE or MRCOG service and programming sources.
