ABQ Ride bus service information and data.

## Rider Information

- [ABQ RIDE: routes and schedules](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/routes-and-schedules)

  The City’s current route, schedule, detour, and trip-planning information. Consult the official service page before a trip because operational details can change.

## Uptown Connect

- [Uptown Connect project page](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/transit-programs-projects/uptown-connect)

  Tracks the City's transit-centered redevelopment of the Uptown Transit Center, including design and construction progress, the transit plaza, affordable and market-rate housing, commercial space, sustainability features, and the anticipated 2026–2028 delivery schedule.

## ABQ RIDE Forward Network Plan

- [Official ABQ RIDE Forward Project Page](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/transit-programs-projects/abq-ride-forward)

  Tracks the finalized bus-network plan, phased implementation, current project status, official reports, route concepts, and project contact information maintained by the City.

- [Final Recovery Network Plan (June 2025)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/transit/abq-ride-forward/final-recovery-network-plan-2025.pdf)

  Defines the finalized route network, frequencies, service spans, access changes, public-input response, and phased next steps for rebuilding and modernizing Albuquerque bus service. [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/documents/forward-final-report-20250604-1.pdf)

- [Final Recovery Network Map (2025)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/transit/abq-ride-forward/final-recovery-network-map-2025.png)

  Maps the finalized ABQ RIDE Forward network by route and frequency, providing a printable overview of planned fixed-route service and demand-response areas. [Official City image](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/documents/final_recovery_network2025.png)

### Planning History

- [Phase III Recovery Network Report (May 2024)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/transit/abq-ride-forward/phase-iii-recovery-network-report-2024.pdf)

  Presents the draft recovery network, proposed route and frequency changes, service assumptions, access analysis, demographic outcomes, and recommendations later refined into the final plan. [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/documents/abq-ride-forward-recovery-network-report-high-res-20240507.pdf)

- [Phase III Engagement Summary (November 2024)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/transit/abq-ride-forward/phase-iii-engagement-summary-2024.pdf)

  Documents community and stakeholder feedback on the draft recovery network, including outreach, survey results, route-specific comments, respondent characteristics, and requested changes. [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/documents/phase-iii-engagement-summary-december-2024.pdf)

- [Phase II Concepts Report (March 2023)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/transit/abq-ride-forward/phase-ii-concepts-report-2023.pdf)

  Compares contrasting high-ridership and high-coverage bus-network concepts, explaining route structures, service tradeoffs, access outcomes, transit markets, and choices considered during redesign. [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/documents/abq-ride-forward-concepts-report-small-file-20230306.pdf)

- [Phase II Engagement Summary (August 2023)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/transit/abq-ride-forward/phase-ii-engagement-summary-2023.pdf)

  Summarizes public preferences between high-ridership and high-coverage concepts, including surveys, focus groups, stakeholder input, demographic analysis, and priorities for evening and weekend service. [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/documents/abq-ride-forward-phase-ii-engagement-summary-august-2023.pdf)

- [Phase I Existing Conditions Report (August 2022)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/transit/abq-ride-forward/phase-i-existing-conditions-report-2022.pdf)

  Analyzes Albuquerque transit markets, network and route performance, ridership, service frequency, access, equity, zero fares, worker shortages, and fundamental network-design choices. [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/documents/abq-choices-report-20220908.pdf)

- [Phase I Engagement Summary (February 2023)](https://files.abqinfo.com/transportation/transit/abq-ride-forward/phase-i-engagement-summary-2022.pdf)

  Records early community priorities and transit tradeoffs gathered through surveys, focus groups, pop-up events, workshops, meetings, presentations, and outreach to riders and underserved populations. [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/documents/abq-ride-forward-phase-i-engagement-summary.pdf)
