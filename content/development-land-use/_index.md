Major development projects, development activity, zoning, land-use regulation, and the public processes that shape Albuquerque's built environment.

This section covers significant residential, commercial, hotel, industrial, institutional, and mixed-use development without attempting to reproduce every routine permit or private filing. It also preserves the plans, regulations, data, and review standards needed to understand how projects advance.

Start with [Projects]({{< relref "projects.md" >}}) for selected development activity and supporting data. For the plans that shaped neighborhoods and reinvestment, see [Metropolitan Redevelopment Plans]({{< relref "redevelopment-plans.md" >}}) and [Area & Sector Plans]({{< relref "area-sector-plans.md" >}}). For regulations and site research, see [Zoning & IDO]({{< relref "zoning-ido.md" >}}) and [Development Process]({{< relref "development-process.md" >}}).
