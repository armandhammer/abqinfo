title: "Zoning & IDO"
---

Current zoning tools and a preserved history of Albuquerque's Integrated Development Ordinance. Historical editions matter when researching prior approvals, regulations, and the transition from sector development plans to the unified code.

## Current Ordinance and Zoning Tools

- [Integrated Development Ordinance — Effective May 6, 2026](https://files.abqinfo.com/housing/zoning-ido/cabq-ido-effective-2026-05-06.pdf)

  The City's current Integrated Development Ordinance establishes zoning, development standards, and review procedures for Albuquerque. This preserved original is the effective May 2026 release.

  [Official City IDO page](https://www.cabq.gov/planning/codes-policies-regulations/integrated-development-ordinance-1/integrated-development-ordinance)

- [Interactive IDO Zoning Map](https://cabq.maps.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=53bf716981b14d25a31e7a2549c2d61b)

  Search an address or parcel and inspect the mapped zoning layer. Map results should be checked against the effective IDO and City review staff for a site-specific determination.

- [City Zoning and Land-Use Maps](https://www.cabq.gov/planning/agis-maps/maps-for-the-public)

  The City's public map library includes the Zone Atlas, address atlas, council districts, land use, neighborhood associations, and printable reference maps.

## Historical Effective IDO Editions

- [Integrated Development Ordinance, Effective April 20, 2026](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/zoning-ido/cabq-ido-effective-2026-04-20.pdf)

  Preserves the consolidated Integrated Development Ordinance effective April 20, 2026, immediately before the May 2026 edition, documenting the zoning and development regulations applicable during that interval.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/IDO/2025_IDO_Update/IDO_2025_Update_Effective-2026-04-20.pdf)

- [Integrated Development Ordinance, Effective April 21, 2025](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/zoning-ido/cabq-ido-effective-2025-04-21.pdf)

  Preserves the consolidated Integrated Development Ordinance effective April 21, 2025, providing the authoritative zoning and development rules in force before the two 2026 effective editions.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/IDO/O-25-73/IDO_2025_Update_Effective.pdf)

- [Integrated Development Ordinance, Effective August 3, 2024](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/zoning-ido/cabq-ido-effective-2024-08-03.pdf)

  Preserves the consolidated Integrated Development Ordinance effective August 3, 2024, recording Albuquerque zoning, subdivision, development-review, and dimensional regulations before the 2025 annual update.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/IDO/2023_IDO_AnnualUpdate/IDO_2023_AnnualUpdate_Effective.pdf)

- [Integrated Development Ordinance, Effective July 27, 2023](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/zoning-ido/cabq-ido-effective-2023-07-27.pdf)

  Preserves the consolidated Integrated Development Ordinance effective July 27, 2023, documenting the City zoning and development rules adopted through the 2022 annual update.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/IDO/2022_IDO_AnnualUpdate/IDO-2022AnnualUpdate-EFFECTIVE-2023-07-27.pdf)

- [Integrated Development Ordinance, Effective December 25, 2022](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/zoning-ido/cabq-ido-effective-2022-12-25.pdf)

  Preserves the consolidated Integrated Development Ordinance effective December 25, 2022, recording the zoning and development regulations adopted through the 2021 annual update.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/IDO/2021_IDO_AnnualUpdate/IDO-2021AnnualUpdate-2022-12-25-Effective.pdf)

- [Integrated Development Ordinance, Effective August 1, 2021](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/zoning-ido/cabq-ido-effective-2021-08-01.pdf)

  Preserves the City-posted consolidated Integrated Development Ordinance effective August 1, 2021, despite its legacy filename, documenting regulations adopted through the 2020 annual update.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/IDO/2020_IDO_AnnualUpdate/IDO-2020AnnualUpdate-2021-07-16-EffectiveDraft.pdf)

- [Integrated Development Ordinance, Effective November 2, 2020](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/zoning-ido/cabq-ido-effective-2020-11-02.pdf)

  Preserves the consolidated Integrated Development Ordinance effective November 2, 2020, providing the authoritative zoning and development rules following the 2019 annual update.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/IDO/IDO-2019-Effective-2020-11-02.pdf)

- [Integrated Development Ordinance, Effective May 17, 2018](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/zoning-ido/cabq-ido-effective-2018-05-17.pdf)

  Preserves Albuquerque's first effective Integrated Development Ordinance, establishing the unified zoning, subdivision, and development-review framework that replaced the former zoning code and sector-plan system.

  [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/IDO/IDO-Effective-2018-05-17.pdf)

[Official IDO Legislative History and Current Source](https://www.cabq.gov/planning/codes-policies-regulations/integrated-development-ordinance-1/integrated-development-ordinance)

The City page remains the source of truth for the current ordinance and records annual-update legislation, adoption documents, and effective editions. The archived copies above preserve the consolidated code as it appeared at important points in time.
