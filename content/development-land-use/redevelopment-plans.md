---
title: "Metropolitan Redevelopment Plans"
---

Plans and historical records for designated Metropolitan Redevelopment Areas, where the City uses public investment and redevelopment tools to support housing, infrastructure, business activity, and neighborhood revitalization.

- [City Metropolitan Redevelopment Areas](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas)

  Maintains the City’s current index of designated areas, maps, plans, goals, and redevelopment information. The archived plans below preserve records that may be amended, renamed, consolidated, or removed from the live collection.

## Historical and Current Area Plans

- [Old Albuquerque High School Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (1984)](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/redevelopment-plans/cabq-old-albuquerque-high-school-mra-plan-1984.pdf)

  Documents the redevelopment framework for the former Albuquerque High School property, coordinating historic preservation, renovation, public improvements, private investment, and reuse of a prominent Huning Highland landmark.

  [Official City redevelopment-area index](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas) · [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/UDD/AlbuquerqueHighSchoolMRA-Plan.pdf)

- [Central/Highland/Upper Nob Hill Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (2005)](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/redevelopment-plans/cabq-central-highland-upper-nob-hill-mra-plan-2005.pdf)

  Guides reinvestment along Central Avenue through business revitalization, housing, higher-density mixed use, pedestrian improvements, neighborhood stabilization, and coordinated public and private development in the Near Heights.

  [Official City redevelopment-area page](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas/central-highland-upper-nob-hill) · [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/UDD/CentralHighlandUpperNobHillMRA-Plan.pdf)

- [Clayton Heights Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Plan (2010)](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/redevelopment-plans/cabq-clayton-heights-mra-plan-2010.pdf)

  Establishes a redevelopment strategy for the Clayton Heights gateway neighborhood, addressing land use, housing, infrastructure, streets, economic conditions, opportunity sites, community priorities, and implementation tools.

  [Official City redevelopment-area index](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas) · [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/UDD/ClaytonLomasCieloMRA-Plan.pdf)

- [Coronado Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Plan (2016)](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/redevelopment-plans/cabq-coronado-mra-plan-2016.pdf)

  Plans environmental remediation, public-right-of-way improvements, and mixed-use redevelopment of the former industrial property, supporting new housing, jobs, retail, entertainment, connectivity, safety, drainage, and a northern gateway identity.

  [Official City redevelopment-area page](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas/coronado) · [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/UDD/MRA/coronado-metropolitan-redevelopment-area-plan.pdf)

- [Del Rey Metropolitan Redevelopment Project Plan (2006)](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/redevelopment-plans/cabq-del-rey-mra-project-plan-2006.pdf)

  Records the negotiated redevelopment plan for the Del Rey area, including project boundaries, housing and land-development conditions, resident coordination, public actions, statutory findings, and implementation responsibilities.

  [Official City redevelopment-area index](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas) · [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/UDD/DelReyMRA-Plan.pdf)

- [South Broadway Neighborhoods Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (1986; Updated Through 2018)](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/redevelopment-plans/cabq-south-broadway-neighborhoods-mra-plan-1986-2018.pdf)

  Preserves the long-running South Broadway redevelopment framework, including neighborhood conditions, housing, commercial corridors, industry, community facilities, transportation, historic character, public improvements, adopted amendments, and implementation history.

  [Official City redevelopment-area page](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas/south-broadway-neighborhoods) · [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/UDD/SouthBroadwayMRA-Plan.pdf)

- [South Barelas Industrial Park Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (1992)](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/redevelopment-plans/cabq-south-barelas-industrial-park-mra-plan-1992.pdf)

  Promotes environmentally compatible industrial growth beside Barelas, covering business expansion, vacant-land development, City property disposition, industrial-park image, local hiring, code enforcement, transportation access, and infrastructure improvements.

  [Official City redevelopment-area page](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas/south-barelas-industrial-park) · [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/UDD/SouthBarelasIndustrialParkMRA-Plan.PDF)

- [St. Joseph Hospital Metropolitan Redevelopment Plan (1979; Updated Through 2018)](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/redevelopment-plans/cabq-st-joseph-hospital-mra-plan-1979-2018.pdf)

  Preserves the redevelopment record for the former St. Joseph Hospital and Civic Auditorium area, including renovation projects, later development areas, amendments, and the 2017 transition from sector plan to retained MRA plan.

  [Official City redevelopment-area index](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas) · [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/UDD/StJosephHospitalMRA-Plan.pdf)

- [Tingley Beach Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Project I Plan (1983)](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/redevelopment-plans/cabq-tingley-beach-mra-project-plan-1983.pdf)

  Preserves the 1983 Project I redevelopment plan for the beach property at West Central Avenue and the Rio Grande, documenting the statutory framework, site conditions, public actions, and proposed reuse.

  [Official City redevelopment-area index](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas) · [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/UDD/TingleyBeachMRA-Plan.pdf)

- [West Central Metropolitan Redevelopment Area Plan (2004)](https://files.abqinfo.com/development-land-use/redevelopment-plans/cabq-west-central-mra-plan-2004.pdf)

  Guides revitalization along West Central through economic development, streetscape and appearance improvements, opportunity-site design, industrial employment, neighborhood retail, mixed-use development, housing, and connections to the Rio Grande Bosque.

  [Official City redevelopment-area page](https://www.cabq.gov/mra/redevelopment-areas/west-central) · [Official City PDF](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/UDD/WestCentralMRA-Plan.pdf)
