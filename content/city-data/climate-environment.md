City and regional climate plans, greenhouse-gas inventories, and durable performance records relevant to Albuquerque.

## Environmental Justice and Local Conditions

- [Mountain View Environmental Inventory and Gap Mapping Analysis](https://www.bernco.gov/public-works/blog/2026/03/12/mountain-view-environmental-inventory-and-gap-mapping-analysis)

  Tracks Bernalillo County's community-focused environmental inventory for Mountain View, consolidating business, permit, contaminant, air, soil, water, property, and regulatory-agency data into a planned public ArcGIS database with a final report expected in 2026.

- [Mountain View Environmental Inventory Project — Community Workshop 2 (2026 Archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/public-works/environment/bernco-mountain-view-environmental-inventory-workshop-2026.pdf)

  Documents the County public workshop, explaining data sources, permits, agency jurisdictions, three live maps, database navigation, community feedback, final deliverables, and the schedule for completing the environmental inventory.

  The workshop's ArcGIS preview currently requires permission and is not linked as a public dashboard. [Official County PDF](https://www.bernco.gov/public-works/wp-content/uploads/sites/76/2026/06/Mountain-View-Workshop-Presentation_May-2026-004.pdf) · [Check the County project page for public release](https://www.bernco.gov/public-works/blog/2026/03/12/mountain-view-environmental-inventory-and-gap-mapping-analysis)

- [Mountain View Environmental Inventory Project — Information Pamphlet (2026 Archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/public-works/environment/bernco-mountain-view-environmental-inventory-pamphlet-2026.pdf)

  Explains the database contents, agency links, pollution-source information, limitations, community role, project timeline, outreach process, and current County and consultant contacts. [Official County PDF](https://www.bernco.gov/public-works/wp-content/uploads/sites/76/2026/03/MV-EIP-Pamphlet-2.pdf)

## Climate Action Plans

- [City of Albuquerque Climate Action Plan (2009)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/climate-environment/cabq-climate-action-plan-2009.pdf)

  Preserves Albuquerque’s first Climate Action Task Force recommendations, covering greenhouse-gas accounting, buildings, renewable energy, neighborhoods, food, waste, behavior change, and transportation before the City’s later equity-centered plan.

  [Official City climate-plan hub](https://www.cabq.gov/sustainability/climate-action-plan) · [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/sustainability/documents/2009-climate-action-plan.pdf)

- [Albuquerque Climate Action Plan (2021)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/climate-environment/cabq-climate-action-plan-2021.pdf)

  Sets Albuquerque’s community-scale climate strategy with equity-centered actions for governance, energy, buildings, transportation, waste, economic development, adaptation, health, and accountability, informed by frontline-community engagement and implementation priorities.

  [Official City climate-plan hub](https://www.cabq.gov/sustainability/climate-action-plan) · [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/sustainability/documents/2021-climate-action-plan.pdf)

- [Albuquerque Metropolitan Statistical Area Priority Climate Action Plan (2024 Archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/climate-environment/albuquerque-msa-priority-climate-action-plan-2024.pdf)

  Presents the 2024 EPA Climate Pollution Reduction Grants Priority Climate Action Plan for the Albuquerque MSA, identifying near-term measures across buildings, renewable energy, transportation, waste, and neighborhoods with regional greenhouse-gas, equity, delivery-agency, and benefits analyses.

  [EPA Priority Climate Action Plan directory](https://www.epa.gov/inflation-reduction-act/priority-climate-action-plans-states-msas-tribes-and-territories) · [Official EPA PDF](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-03/cprg-pcap-albuquerque-msa-city-of-albuquerque.pdf)

## 2026 Climate Plan Update Foundations

- [Albuquerque Business-As-Usual Emissions Forecast (2025 Archived Workbook)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/climate-environment/cabq-business-as-usual-emissions-forecast-2025.xlsx)

  Models Albuquerque-area community greenhouse-gas emissions through 2050 under a business-as-usual scenario, with transparent assumptions and calculations for transportation, building energy, waste, industrial processes, land use, population, employment, emission factors, and policy baselines.

  [Official City workbook](https://www.cabq.gov/sustainability/documents/abq_only_bau_08072025.xlsx) · [Official City climate-plan hub](https://www.cabq.gov/sustainability/climate-action-plan)

- [Central New Mexico Climate Workforce Analysis (2025 Archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/climate-environment/central-nm-climate-workforce-analysis-2025.pdf)

  Assesses priority clean-energy and transportation occupations, projected growth, workforce gaps, training providers, state and regional programs, and access for disadvantaged communities while recommending stronger coordination across Central New Mexico’s workforce-development system.

  [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/sustainability/documents/ccap_workforce-analysis_accessible.pdf) · [Official City climate-plan hub](https://www.cabq.gov/sustainability/climate-action-plan)

- [Central New Mexico Climate Funding and Finance Overview (2025 Archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/climate-environment/central-nm-climate-funding-finance-overview-2025.pdf)

  Consolidates scattered federal, state, local, utility, banking, bond, grant, philanthropic, public-private, and technical-assistance resources for climate projects, with Albuquerque-area examples and practical guidance for turning regional climate strategies into funded implementation.

  [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/sustainability/documents/ccap_funding-finance-overview_accessible.pdf) · [Official City climate-plan hub](https://www.cabq.gov/sustainability/climate-action-plan)

## UNM Albuquerque Campus Sustainability

- [UNM Sustainability Strategic Plan 2025-2030 (Archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/climate-environment/unm-sustainability-strategic-plan-2025-2030.pdf)

  Sets a five-year program for UNM's Albuquerque campus covering energy and greenhouse-gas reductions, water and land use, waste, transportation, food, applied learning, engagement, communications, governance, measurable actions, responsible departments, and implementation metrics.

  [Current UNM Strategic Plan Page](https://sustainability.unm.edu/engagement/sustainability-strategic-plan.html) · [Official UNM PDF](https://sustainability.unm.edu/assets/unm-sustainability_strategic-plan_2025-30.pdf)

- [UNM Greenhouse Gas Inventory FY2025 Update (Archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/climate-environment/unm-greenhouse-gas-inventory-fy2025-update.pdf)

  Updates Albuquerque Main Campus Scope 1 and 2 emissions through FY2025, documenting a 15% annual decline, progress from the FY2006 baseline, campus-energy trends, calculation methods, reduction targets, and the effect of cogeneration-equipment problems on the reported result.

  [Current UNM Greenhouse-Gas Page](https://sustainability.unm.edu/campus/emissions.html) · [Official UNM PDF](https://sustainability.unm.edu/campus/fy-2025-ghg-report.pdf)

- [UNM Greenhouse Gas Inventory FY2024 (Archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/climate-environment/unm-greenhouse-gas-inventory-fy2024.pdf)

  Establishes the detailed Albuquerque Main Campus emissions baseline, defining inventory boundaries and methods, quantifying natural gas, fleet fuel, and purchased electricity, comparing FY2006-FY2024 results, and explaining the buildings, utilities, and state targets shaping future decarbonization.

  [Current UNM Greenhouse-Gas Page](https://sustainability.unm.edu/campus/emissions.html) · [Official UNM PDF](https://sustainability.unm.edu/assets/unm-sustainability_greenhouse-gas-inventory_2024.pdf)
