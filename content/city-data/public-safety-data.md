title: "Public Safety Data"
---

Maintained public-safety dashboards, police statistics, geographic breakdowns, reporting-method documentation, and historical trend reports. Current figures are preliminary and may change as cases and classifications are updated.

## Current Dashboards and Datasets

- [ABQ Safety Dashboard](https://www.cabq.gov/abq-view/abq-dashboard/safety)

  The City dashboard brings together public-safety measures and reporting in one maintained location. Use it for current, high-level performance information rather than treating a downloaded snapshot as current.

- [Albuquerque Police Crime Statistics](https://www.cabq.gov/police/crime-statistics)

  Provides APD’s current monthly snapshot, crime trends, homicides, calls for service, arrests, traffic reporting, officer-involved-shooting records, and links to crime mapping and detailed statistical series.

- [Albuquerque NIBRS Crime Statistics](https://www.cabq.gov/police/crime-statistics/nibrs)

  Explains APD’s National Incident-Based Reporting System methodology and maintains current violent, property, and society crime reports, including citywide and quarterly comparisons.

- [APD Crime Statistics by Area Command](https://www.cabq.gov/police/crime-statistics/crime-stats-by-area-command)

  Organizes recent crime statistics geographically by APD area command, making neighborhood-scale patterns easier to locate than in citywide totals alone.

## Historical Crime Trends

- [Crime in Albuquerque 2017–2022 (archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/public-safety/cabq-crime-statistics-2017-2022.pdf)

  Preserves APD’s six-year comparison of violent and property crime from 2017 through 2022, with category-level trends, reporting-method explanations, fixed-point-in-time limitations, and FBI-derived historical data.

  [Official APD PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/police/documents/crime-stats-2017-2022-final-16mar2023.pdf) · [Current APD crime statistics](https://www.cabq.gov/police/crime-statistics)
