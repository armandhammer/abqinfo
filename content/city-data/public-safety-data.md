title: "Public Safety Data"
---

Police, fire, and emergency services data.

## Current dashboards and datasets

- [ABQ Safety Dashboard](https://www.cabq.gov/abq-view/abq-dashboard/safety)

  The City dashboard brings together public-safety measures and reporting in one maintained location. Use it for current, high-level performance information rather than treating a downloaded snapshot as current.

- [Police incidents open data](https://data.cabq.gov/publicsafety/policeincidents/)

  The City publishes police-incident data and machine-readable formats through its open-data service. Definitions, update timing, and appropriate use are described with the dataset.
