---
title: "Budget & Spending"
---

Adopted budgets show what Albuquerque plans to collect and spend; audited financial reports show what actually occurred. Use both when comparing appropriations, revenues, expenditures, fund balances, debt, and long-term financial condition.

## Current Budget

- [City of Albuquerque Approved Budget, Fiscal Year 2027](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/budget-spending/cabq-fy2027-approved-budget.pdf)

  The City of Albuquerque's adopted Fiscal Year 2027 budget provides citywide revenue, appropriations, department and program details, capital support, performance information, and summary charts explaining where public funds come from and how they are allocated.

  [Official City budget archive](https://www.cabq.gov/dfa/budget/annual-budget)

## Audited Financial Results

- [City of Albuquerque Annual Comprehensive Financial Report, Fiscal Year 2025 (archived PDF)](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/budget-spending/cabq-annual-comprehensive-financial-report-fy2025.pdf)

  Presents Albuquerque’s independently audited financial statements for the year ended June 30, 2025, including fund balances, revenues, expenditures, debt, pensions, capital assets, enterprise operations, statistical trends, and budget-to-actual schedules.

  [Official City PDF](https://www.cabq.gov/dfa/documents/agency-6004-city-of-albuquerque-fy25-acfr-final-web.pdf) · [Official City financial-report archive](https://www.cabq.gov/dfa/treasury/investor-information/annual-comprehensive-financial-reports)

## Five-Year Financial Forecasts

The City publishes this forecast before developing each annual budget. Together, the editions preserve changing expectations for revenue, spending, economic conditions, and future budget pressure. [The official forecast archive](https://www.cabq.gov/dfa/budget/five-year-forecast) remains the source of truth for later releases.

- [City of Albuquerque Five-Year Forecast, Fiscal Year 2026](https://files.abqinfo.com/city-data/budget-spending/cabq-five-year-forecast-fy2026.pdf)

  Preserves the City's fiscal year 2026 five-year outlook for General Fund revenues, expenditures, economic conditions, budget pressures, alternative scenarios, and the assumptions informing annual budget development.
