title: "Demographics"
---

Current population, household, economic, neighborhood, and regional-growth data for Albuquerque and its metropolitan context.

## Albuquerque Data

- [U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts: Albuquerque](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/albuquerquecitynewmexico/BZA010223)

  Provides the Census Bureau’s maintained city-level estimates and statistics for population, age, race and ethnicity, housing, households, language, education, disability, employment, income, poverty, commuting, and business activity.

- [Albuquerque Neighborhood Data](https://data.cabq.gov/community/neighborhoods/)

  The City’s open-data service provides neighborhood geography and associated data resources for map-based analysis, local reference, and comparisons among Albuquerque communities.

## Regional Data

- [MRCOG Socioeconomic Data](https://www.mrcog-nm.gov/150/Socioeconomic-Data)

  Mid-Region Council of Governments publishes regional population, households, employment, income, commuting, and related planning data. It is the appropriate regional source when a question extends beyond City limits.

- [MRCOG Community Profiles](https://www.mrcog-nm.gov/165/Community-Profiles)

  Summarizes the central New Mexico region's population, geography, counties, municipalities, pueblos, transportation connections, and urban-rural context, providing a concise companion to detailed census and socioeconomic datasets.

- [MRMPO Socioeconomic Forecasts](https://www.mrcog-nm.gov/227/Socioeconomic-Forecasts)

  Provides downloadable population, housing, and employment estimates and forecasts through 2045 for counties and small-area Data Analysis Subzones used in regional transportation and land-use planning.
