title: "Economy & Workforce"
---

Regional economic-development plans, business resources, workforce services, and employment information.

- [MRCOG Economic Development](https://www.mrcog-nm.gov/355/Economic-Development)

  Links the regional Comprehensive Economic Development Strategy, business resources, interagency coordination, and economic-development funding assistance, with examples of infrastructure and community investments in Albuquerque and the surrounding region.

- [Workforce Connection of Central New Mexico](https://www.mrcog-nm.gov/354/Workforce)

  Provides career and employer services for Bernalillo and three neighboring counties, including job-search and resume help, skills assessment, training, applicant recruitment, job-fair support, and regional workforce planning.
