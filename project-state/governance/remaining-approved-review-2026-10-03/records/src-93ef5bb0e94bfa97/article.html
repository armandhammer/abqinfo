PROJECT SCOPE

Bernalillo County is installing thousands of feet of fiber-optic cable to establish its own infrastructure for voice and data communications.  The fiber-optic cable provides a greater bandwidth to carry digital information. Individual fiber-optic lines are strands of optically pure glass as thin as a human hair that can carry the digital information over the long distances.

Installation locations:

Animal Care Facility – from Public Works to the Animal Care and Resource Center on Second St. | $250,000, in construction

Fire Station on Price Street and then down to the Fire Training Center on Prosperity and Broadway and Mountain View Community Center | $300,000 (estimate), in design phase
