PROJECT SCOPE Bernalillo County is installing thousands of feet of fiber-optic cable to establish its own infrastructure for voice and data communications.  The fiber-optic cable provides a greater bandwidth to carry digital information. Individual fiber-optic lines are strands of optically pure glass as thin as a human hair that can carry the digital information over the long distances.
