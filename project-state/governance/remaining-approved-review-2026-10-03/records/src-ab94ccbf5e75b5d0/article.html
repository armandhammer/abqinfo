Project Scope

Creation of Tiny Home Village, including new community building with kitchen and bathrooms, up to 30 homes, site work, and selective renovation of existing structure

Funding: 2016 Public Housing General Obligation Bond – $2 million; 2018 General Obligation Bond – $750,000; NM State Capital Outlay – $595,000; BernCo ESGRT Grant – $207,816; COA – $75,000; Total – $3,627,816
