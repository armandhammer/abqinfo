Project Scope The design of Mountain View Roadway and Drainage improvements has been completed. The limits of the project area are Rio Bravo Boulevard to the north, South Diversion Channel to the south and east, and the Barr Main Canal (BMC) to the west. The project has 4 major phases and will be constructed as funding becomes available. Phase 1A will construct the downstream drainage pond that collects stormwater from areas east of 2nd Street.
