Project Scope: This project includes installation of approximately 1240 LF of new sanitary sewer pipe and manholes along the future Ladera Rd from intersection of Comfort Way to Atrisco Vista and installation of 2,240 LF along Atrisco Vista Blvd from Ladera Rd. going south and tie into an existing sanitary sewer manhole.

Funding: This project is comprised of Bernalillo County District 1 Bonds.
