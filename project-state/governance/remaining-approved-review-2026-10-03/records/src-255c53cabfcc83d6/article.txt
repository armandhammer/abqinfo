This facility is located at 2008 Larrazolo Road SE PROJECT SCOPE A new 3,250 square foot building addition to the South Valley Multipurpose Center is currently in design. The new addition will be used for half-court basketball and many other appropriate activities. An emergency generator and automatic transfer switch will be installed so that the new facility can function as a shelter location during emergencies. Schedule This project is being designed by Vigil & Associates Architectural Group and is currently in design. Construction is anticipated to begin in March 2013. Current Status: Completed.
