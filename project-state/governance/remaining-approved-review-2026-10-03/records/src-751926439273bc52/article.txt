Scope This project installed asphalt paving on 6,925 linear feet of existing gravel roads in the East Mountains area. The paved 1,760 linear feet Oakhill Drive/Sharp Road that serves the Sandia Knolls subdivision was pulverized and new asphalt placed. The gravel roads that were paved are Geer Road, Hobart Road, Gilbert Place, Jennifer Drive, and Olive Road. General Project Information: Status | Completed Section| Brad Catanach, P.E.
