<h2>2nd Street Southwest Corridor</h2>

<div class="group"><!-- start .group -->
	<div id="project-main"><!-- start #project-main -->

<h3 class="first">Project Description</h3>
<p>The Federal Highway Administration, Central Federal Lands Highway Division (FHWA-CFLHD), in cooperation with the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS) and Bernalillo County, are proposing improvements to 2nd Street Southwest in Albuquerque, New Mexico. The portion of 2nd Street Southwest identified for improvements is located between the Valle de Oro National Wildlife Refuge Visitor Center Entrance and the South Diversion Channel; a distance of approximately 1.5 miles.</p>

<h3>Key Project Dates</h3>
<p>
	Advertise Date: Summer 2017<br>
	Award Letter Date: Fall 2017<br>
	Construction Date: Fall 2017<br>
	Estimated Completion Date: Fall 2018
</p>
