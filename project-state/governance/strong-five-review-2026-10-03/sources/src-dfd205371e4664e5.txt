Project Scope Bernalillo County is undertaking a series of improvements to the Paradise Hills Community Center and nearby facilities, including Paradise Hills Park, Park Lane, and Paradise Hills Pool. A phased suite of improvements proposed for the center and nearby facilities were rated a “Very High Priority” under the Bernalillo County Parks, Recreation and Open Space Facilities Plan (PROS Plan), which was adopted by the County Commission in October 2015. These improvements were initially identified during a master planning process during the fall and winter of 2013. State legislators have provided approximately $800,000 for the first  phase of the project.? – July 27, 2016 Phase 0: Senior Annex Trail, Parking and Landscape Improvements Construction completed – Summer 2015 Improvements included Rx prescription trail, native landscaping, parking and drainage improvements Phase 1: Redesign of Paradise Hills Park and Park Lane Engineering, traffic study and landscape design kicked off – May 2016 Potential Improvements include: Mid-block speed table (to slow traffic down and ensure safe crossing of kids from the center to the park) & drop off area for center and pool;
