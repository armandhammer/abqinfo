**Lead Avenue and Coal Avenue Rest-in-Red Feasibility (2021-07-27; historical report)**

Evaluates rest-in-red signal operation on Lead and Coal avenues using 2021 speed counts, crash history, signal equipment, costs, and intersection priorities. Recommends starting during off-peak hours; this historical feasibility study does not establish present-day implementation or effectiveness.

Original source: https://www.cabq.gov/council/documents/lead-coal-rest-in-red-7-28-2021-final-1.pdf

**New Vision for Lead & Coal Avenues: A Quality of Life Document (2007-03-23; historical report)**

Presents the 2007 task force’s one-way and two-way redesign proposals, supported by corridor conditions, cross-sections, costs, and planning-level traffic analysis. Includes Wilson & Company’s separate recommendation for the one-way option; these proposals are not an adopted construction plan.
