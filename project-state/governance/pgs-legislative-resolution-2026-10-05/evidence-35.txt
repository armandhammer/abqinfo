10

11
as “a description of the service area and projections of changes in land uses,
12
densities, intensities and population in the service area over at least a five-
13
year period.”  The Act states that the City must prepare a capital
14
improvements plan that addresses, among other requirements, the total
15
number of projected service units necessitated by and attributable to new
16
development within the service area based on the approved land use
17
assumptions
and

with

18
engineering or planning criteria.  The Impact Fee Committee has advised and
19
assisted the Council regarding the adoption of the land use assumptions.  The
20
City has met the notice and hearing requirements of the Act for the adoption
21
of land use assumptions.
