## Summary

Adds three individually identified historical PGS enactments beneath the existing Planned Growth Strategy study on Area & Sector Plans. The study remains historical planning analysis: these ordinances identify specific measures the City enacted, without implying that the whole study became law or that these originals are current consolidated law.
