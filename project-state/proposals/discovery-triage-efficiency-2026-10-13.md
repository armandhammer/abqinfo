# Owner instruction — efficient discovery and review of unfamiliar documents

**Status:** owner-directed, scoped workflow-change specification, staged for governed integration. **No new R2 upload, visitor-visible publication, or substantive inventory disposition is authorized by this specification alone.** The existing active registry, settled decisions, family/consolidation rules, review gates, storage policy and owner content-PR merge gate remain controlling except where explicitly superseded under the governance workflow.

## Objective

Reduce *model usage per useful accepted document*, not just elapsed time or pages rendered. Keep ordinary unfamiliar-document discovery largely autonomous. Offer the owner **at most 3–5 genuinely editorial borderline candidates per daily review batch when useful**, not a quota and not a prerequisite for high-confidence automation. Ordinary source recovery, provenance, version/finality, duplicate assessment, eligibility exclusions and clear publication-quality judgments remain Codex's responsibility. Do not turn an established clear exclusion into a human-review question.

## Graduated review path

**0. Cheap, read-only discovery screen (bulk, deterministic).** Gather authoritative listing/title/URL/publisher/date/format and discover context. Check known URL/title/identifier/hash matches, existing inventory/site/family references and obvious mission exclusions. Avoid model-reading full documents or doing expensive retrieval for unpromising leads. Structural and safe source retrieval may be used when needed to establish what a record actually is; don't advance any inventory status across the standing positive mission-scope gate without the required assessment. Record concise screen outcomes: `obvious_exclusion` (with evidence), `exact_known_duplicate`, `promising`, `uncertain`, or `unretrievable`. Do not label semantic near-duplicates as exact unless bytes match. Never discard a candidate on title alone where substantive content could change the conclusion.

**1. Focused substantive screen (promising candidates).** Use existing PDF/other-format extraction/validation. Check file integrity, encryption, length, page tree, readable text presence, structure and missing/blank indicators mechanically (all pages where cheap), without sending each page to the model. Read/render a relevant *representative selection*: cover/title, summary or contents, materially relevant findings and maps/tables, conclusion and selected appendices. Default to no more than ~4–12 selected pages for an ordinary long PDF as a starting heuristic, **not a completeness claim, hard ceiling or override of an applicable stricter rule**. For very short, mostly graphical, scanned, complex, anomalous or weakly understood files inspect enough material to support a defensible decision; escalate where necessary. Record pages/methods reviewed and evidence limits. Batch metadata/cheap extraction before model decisions to minimize repetitive context and round trips.

**2. Focused AI quality and family decision.** Assess actual Albuquerque relevance (both existing mission gates), material visitor usefulness, unique substantive content, provenance, adoption/version/finality, currentness/supersession, original-vs-wrapper relationships, appropriate standalone vs consolidated family role, proposed site section and description. Use targeted inventory/family queries rather than whole-inventory reads, and retain structured `scope_assessment` and `quality_assessment` as required by AGENTS. Clearly unsuitable cases can be excluded under existing governed dispositions; unambiguous eligible records can proceed automatically through normal existing review/archive/PR gates when separately authorized. Insufficient inspection is a reason to examine more, not to invent a confident result.

**3. Comprehensive escalation only as needed.** Deep reading, full-page visual review, source/version recovery, comparative family analysis or broader searches are reserved for genuine contradictions, damaged/complex documents, ambiguous status, near-duplicate substantive questions, and tasks with stricter active governance. No arbitrary limit on necessary investigations.

## Human editorial shortlist

Send only **genuine publication-value/mission-scope borderline** decisions whose remaining uncertainty is judgment, not facts Codex can research. Reuse the existing exclusive `mission_scope_borderline` queue for actual mission-scope borderline decisions and preserve its schema and standing 20-case rule; do **not** introduce false reasons or misclassify quality/provenance/finality questions as mission-scope borderline. If a separate editorial quality shortlist requires a new state/queue, first explicitly propose and register that scoped policy change; don't silently repurpose existing fields.

A shortlist item is compact: exact title/year/publisher, page count and verified official/archive/source link, 2–3 sentences of substantive content, unique Albuquerque value and overlap, proposed placement/form, evidence inspected, specific unresolved editorial question, and Codex's recommendation. Offer `Include / Exclude / Investigate` with no unsupported approval semantics. Cap normal owner-facing batches to 3–5 per day *at most*, without automating delivery or blocking autonomous work on clear cases. Owner judgment settles editorial disposition only; it is not independent R2-upload permission, proof of document validity or authority to merge a visitor-visible PR. Record the decision in durable governance before acting; never re-ask settled questions.

## Bounded pilot and measurement

Implement this as a **small amendment to the existing ordinary-discovery/background-review procedures**, with at most one necessary reusable deterministic helper, **not** a new framework, custom per-document scripts, test-suite rewrite or expansive repository cleanup. Resolve complete active governance for the exact workflow mutation; register precise authorized supersessions only where the above actually conflicts with active rules. Leave owner-supplied fast path intact. Don't mutate R2, site content or unrelated records.

Run a **read-only pilot of up to 24 previously undecided unfamiliar candidates**, drawn from a frozen, explicitly identified available discovery population (not prior settled/held families), unless doing so would breach a governance gate. Stage 0 screen all; focused-review an appropriately selected subset, including some screened-out cases as a false-negative check. Produce a shortlist only if genuine editorials arise, maximum five; otherwise say none. Save a compact report: candidate IDs, stages reached, sampled/inspected pages, outcomes/evidence, escalation reasons, elapsed time and actual model token/usage readings **only if measurable**, estimated work avoided clearly labeled as estimates, and any missed high-value candidate observed in spot audit. Pilot findings are provisional, **not binding inventory exclusions or publication approvals**. Stop and report exact blocker if an active instruction prevents a read-only test.

Use existing full project validation for actual policy/tool changes, plus focused negative cases proving no scope/quality/family/governance shortcuts, no unauthorized R2/visible changes, no invented human gates, and no automatic content merge. Prefer reuse of existing test infrastructure. Record results in CURRENT and a concise receipt. Background-only integration is permitted under standing rules when checks pass; verify `main` and `chatgpt/planning-snapshot` actual remote refs.

## Usage and stopping discipline

Do not consume a whole usage block on architecture. Work in checkpoints: (A) record authority and the minimal procedure, (B) bounded pilot/report, (C) focused validation and integration. Stop after the intended work. Do **not** expand scope to historical campaign reconciliation, source recovery, R2 archival, full-page inspection of every pilot PDF, or tangential test repair. On an expensive blocker, save a checkpoint and report it rather than repeatedly regenerating contracts or building new infrastructure.
