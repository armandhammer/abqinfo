# Enforceable publication-quality lifecycle

Publication value is a separate decision from mission scope, source authenticity, checksum identity, successful archiving, visual rendering and link validation. `PublicationQuality.py` evaluates the actual inventory record's durable `publication_quality_decision`; validated status is not a quality approval.

Before any new or changed record can enter/remain in implemented or validated status, the inventory update tools run the actual-record gate. Visible implementation validators explicitly call `validate_affected_records`. Completed production audits use `validate_completed_implementation`: they still evaluate each current record and can report only exact, unchanged, sealed remediation debt; without a completed production closeout they require every actual candidate to pass. A sealed historical workflow stage validates its original historical population and evidence and cannot authorize a current publication transition.

The complete normal suite audits all current visible implemented/validated inventory records by matching their actual URLs throughout content, rather than trusting saved placement fields. It also checks changed implemented/validated inventory records against the adoption baseline even when no content link exists. Every failure must either be remediated with an evidence-backed passing decision or remain exactly within the enumerated legacy debt witness. The witness is not an approval, not grandfathering and not a switch that disables the gate. New debt, changed unresolved inventory rows, new placements or increased link counts fail. Subsequent cleanup stages may shrink the debt set, but must preserve its original historical witness and their own exact-delta safeguards.

Assessments address document function, substantive content, durable usefulness, information density, uniqueness, series/component relationships, publication form, currentness when relevant and concrete rationale. Every 1–2-page standalone document or otherwise limited record requires a concrete limited-content assessment, including specific substance, unique durable use, why shortness suffices and evidence. No page-count ban applies to useful signed instruments, maps, tables or concise findings. Dated guidelines, regulations, criteria, procedures, checklists, fees and operational references require an authoritative currentness/supersession finding and publication qualification.

Public engagement is evaluated by function. Raw charts, official data, participant counts and distributions alone do not establish standalone value. An engagement assessment must identify analysis, formal findings/action or other durable unique information. No minimum-participant threshold applies. Components must conform to an evidence-backed aggregation decision; a standalone exception must establish distinct value.

Negative/questionable September 13 findings and the owner's Old Town rejection remain durable contradictions until explicitly superseded by identified findings and new evidence. Future negative decisions must be preserved as historical evidence with stable finding IDs, rather than overwritten when a new assessment reverses them. Corrections advance through sealed historical stages and new exact-delta guards; they never rewrite the original evidence.
