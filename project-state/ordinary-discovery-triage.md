# Ordinary discovery: tiered review supplement

This is the small ordinary-discovery/background-review amendment authorized by the [October 13 owner specification](proposals/discovery-triage-efficiency-2026-10-13.md), registered as `workflow-ordinary-discovery-triage`. It supplements [campaign review](campaign-workflow.md); it does not launch a campaign. The [owner-supplied fast path](owner-supplied-document-workflow.md) remains intact. Complete registry resolution, settled decisions, stricter applicable review rules and all existing publication safeguards still control.

1. **Freeze once and screen cheaply.** Identify an available, previously undecided population and freeze exact IDs and anticipated artifacts before review. Resolve the complete registry and pin the immutable contract; use its per-record queries and freshness checks. Keep settled/held families out of a read-only pilot. Batch authoritative listing context, title, URL, publisher, date and format, then targeted inventory/site/family URL, identifier, title and available hash comparisons. Record `obvious_exclusion`, `exact_known_duplicate`, `promising`, `uncertain` or `unretrievable` with evidence. A title or semantic resemblance alone cannot establish exclusion or an exact duplicate: substantive context supports exclusions; exact duplicate files require matching bytes. Screen outcomes are provisional evidence, not inventory dispositions, eligibility approvals or backlog ranking.
2. **Inspect promising material selectively.** Reuse `scripts/project/extract_pdf.py`, `Get-PdfMetadata.ps1` and existing format-specific rendering tools. Choose an installed interpreter with the required dependencies; do not install tooling for this procedure. Mechanically check integrity, encryption, length/page tree, text/image presence and blank/missing indicators across all pages where cheap, without feeding every page to the model. Batch extraction before model decisions. For an ordinary long PDF start with roughly 4–12 relevant pages: title/cover, summary/contents, material findings/maps/tables, ending and selected appendices. Record exact inspected pages, methods and limits. This is a heuristic, not a ceiling or completeness claim. Short, graphical, scanned, damaged, anomalous, complex or poorly understood records need enough additional inspection to support the decision; an applicable stricter rule takes precedence. Automated checks do not count as visual inspection.
3. **Assess substance and complete families.** Read the actual material for both mission gates, visitor usefulness, unique content and overlap, provenance, adoption/version/finality, currentness, wrapper/original relationships, family role, placement and description. Use targeted family queries, retain the required structured scope and quality assessments, and preserve prior exclusions and settled decisions. Scope, authenticity, extraction, archive readiness and consolidation do not substitute for publication quality. Apply the existing limited-content exception requirements to short sparse documents. Ordinary factual research and clear exclusions remain Codex's responsibility. A separately authorized disposition uses existing governed inventory tools; a pilot applies none.
4. **Escalate only for a specific reason.** Contradictions, damaged/complex files, ambiguous finality, substantive near-duplicates, weak understanding or stricter governance justify deeper source/version/family research or full-page visual review. Investigate as much as necessary; insufficient inspection cannot support a confident decision. This procedure grants no source-recovery, R2, inventory or publication authority beyond the current task.

Only genuinely editorial borderline questions belong in an owner shortlist. The existing exclusive `mission_scope_borderline` queue and 20-case rule remain controlling; do not misclassify factual, provenance, finality or quality questions. No new quality queue/state is created. Clear cases continue autonomously through separately authorized existing gates. Offer at most 3–5 useful items per daily owner review batch, never a quota or automatic delivery. Each item records exact title/year/publisher, page count, verified official/source/archive link, 2–3 sentences of substance, unique Albuquerque value and overlap, proposed placement/form, inspected evidence, precise unresolved editorial question and recommendation. `Include / Exclude / Investigate` settles only editorial judgment; register the owner decision before acting and never re-ask a settled question. It does not authorize upload or content merge.

For the bounded read-only pilot, freeze up to 24 available previously undecided unfamiliar candidates. Screen all, then focused-review a selected subset and some screened-out cases as a false-negative audit. Save IDs, stages, outcomes/evidence, exact pages/methods, escalation reasons, elapsed time, observed missed high-value candidates and at most five genuine shortlist items (otherwise none). Record actual model tokens/usage only when a meter is available; label estimated work avoided as estimates. Without accepted documents or usage readings, do not claim reduced model usage per accepted document. If no eligible population is available, record that prerequisite blocker; zero candidates is not a completed pilot. Do not reopen holds or expand into a new crawl/source-recovery campaign to manufacture pilot membership.

Checkpoint the core amendment to GitHub before pilot and validation work. Use existing full project validation for policy/tool changes and existing focused negative coverage for scope, quality, family/governance, external-action and content-merge gates. Verify inventory and visitor-visible paths unchanged. Background-only integration follows existing policy after checks pass and verifies actual remote `main` and `chatgpt/planning-snapshot` refs. R2 operations, visitor-visible changes, substantive inventory dispositions and automatic content merge are outside this task. Preserve progress and stop on a systemic blocker; do not repair unrelated historical workflows. No new framework, custom per-document script, queue or helper is needed.
